Food Packaging Guide

How to Read a Nutrition Facts Label

Before buying packaged snacks, check the serving size and calories per serving at the top of the label. This helps you know the exact energy you are getting from the whole package.

Healthy Restaurant Habits

Eating Out: How to Make Smart Meal Choices

Restaurant meals often contain hidden calories from oils, sauces, and large sides. Making simple swaps—like choosing grilled dishes, ordering a fresh salad side, and drinking water—keeps your meal balanced and light.

The Healthy Plate Method

Why Portion Size Matters

Eating too much of any food adds extra calories to your day. Dividing your plate into clear sections for vegetables, protein, and carbs helps you control portions without needing to weigh your food.
