Group Experience

Case Study: A University Student Group

A group of university students used GlowCal for two weeks to track and review their daily lunch choices on campus.

Steps They Followed:

  1. Checked nutrition labels on packaged drinks and snacks before buying.
  2. Compared regular meal sizes with large combo meals using GlowCal guides.
  3. Followed the balanced plate portions during lunch breaks.

Two-Week Comparison Results:

Daily Habit Before GlowCal After Two Weeks
Drink Choice Sugary soft drinks every day Water or fresh juice without added sugar
Meal Portion Size Always ordered large combo meals Chose regular size with extra salad
Nutrition Awareness Guessed calories without checking Checked labels and serving sizes regularly
